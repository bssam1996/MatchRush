# Match Rush

Match Rush is a symbol-matching game for Android. Find the one symbol shared
between your card and the centre card, then race to clear your stack.

## Play your way

- **Solo practice:** play offline without an account.
- **Private multiplayer:** create a room, share its code and play with 2-6 players.
- **Game setup:** choose 3, 4, 6, 8, 9 or 10 symbols per card and a game length.
- **Live matches:** synchronized cards, countdowns, placements and per-match statistics.
- **Rematches and reconnection:** play another round or return to an interrupted match.
- **Guest protection:** link a guest to Google to keep the same player identity.
- **Feedback and accessibility:** symbol artwork, sounds, haptics, reduced motion
  and layouts that support larger text.

Three incorrect taps on one card trigger a clearly displayed three-second penalty.

## Accounts

Accounts are optional. On Android, use **Continue with Google** to create or
restore an account. Choose **Protect guest with Google** to preserve an existing
guest's identity and saved multiplayer data. Recover it on another phone by
signing in with the same Google account.

Guest multiplayer is available when enabled on the game server. An unprotected
guest cannot be recovered after its saved session is lost. Leave the current
room before switching accounts or signing out.

Email/password signup and recovery are deferred. The current account flow uses
Google and does not require an SMTP service.
