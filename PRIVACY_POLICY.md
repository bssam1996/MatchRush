# Match Rush Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 October 2026

Match Rush (Android application ID `com.bplusplus.matchrush`) is provided by
Bassam Abozaid. This policy explains how we handle information when you use
Match Rush. For privacy questions or requests, contact
[bssam2012@gmail.com](mailto:bssam2012@gmail.com).

## Information we handle

### Solo gameplay

Solo gameplay runs on your device and does not require an account. Solo moves
and scores are not uploaded to our multiplayer game server.

### Google accounts and guests

If you choose Google sign-in, Google and our authentication provider, Supabase,
process your sign-in. Account information can include your Google account
identifier, email address, name, profile picture and verification information
made available through Google's sign-in process. We use this information to
identify your account, restore access and link an existing guest when requested.
We do not receive your Google password or request access to your Gmail messages,
contacts or Google Drive files.

When guest multiplayer is available, it creates a guest account with a unique
player identifier and a generated display name. Linking it to Google keeps that
same player identifier. An unprotected guest may become inaccessible if its
saved session is lost.

Email/password signup and recovery are not offered in the current app. A Google
email address can still be stored as part of Google authentication.

### Multiplayer and service operation

For online play, we store information needed to run the game, including:

- Player identifiers, display names and account/profile timestamps.
- Rooms, room settings, membership, readiness and connection status.
- Match assignments, submitted moves, correct and incorrect taps, response
  times, penalties, completion status, placements and per-match statistics.
- Request timing and limits used to protect the service from misuse.

Our hosting and authentication services also receive connection information
such as IP addresses, request timestamps and technical/error logs when you use
online features. If you contact us, we receive your contact information and
whatever information you include in your message.

### Information stored on your device

The app stores authentication session information so you can return without
signing in every time. It also stores temporary Google sign-in request metadata
so an interrupted browser sign-in can resume. Gameplay and feedback settings
are held in app memory for the current session. Clearing app data or uninstalling
removes local app information, but does not automatically delete server accounts.

The current app has no advertising, advertising identifiers or separate
advertising/analytics SDK. It does not request access to your precise location,
contacts, camera, microphone or personal photo library.

## How we use information

We use information to authenticate players, protect and recover guest accounts,
create and synchronize multiplayer rooms, process moves and results, restore
interrupted games, respond to support requests and maintain service security.
We do not sell your personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

Where applicable data-protection law requires a lawful basis, providing the
requested online game service and account features supports performance of our
service agreement; preventing abuse and maintaining reliability support our
legitimate interests; and applicable legal requirements may require processing.
When a feature requires consent, you can choose whether to use it and can withdraw
that consent by contacting us or stopping use of that feature.

## Who receives information

Other players in your room can see your player identifier, display name,
membership/readiness status and shared game results or performance information.
The game's room views do not expose your Google email address or authentication
tokens to other players.

We use Supabase for hosted authentication, database storage and multiplayer
synchronization. Google provides the optional sign-in service. These providers
process information needed to deliver and protect their services. See
[Supabase's privacy information](https://supabase.com/privacy) and
[Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

We may disclose information when required by applicable law or a valid legal
request, or when necessary to protect players and the security of the service.
Service providers may process information in countries other than your own,
subject to their applicable processing arrangements and safeguards.

## Retention and security

Account information is retained while needed to provide access and recovery.
Multiplayer records are kept as needed for room operation, reconnection, results
and service security. A room becoming inaccessible or expiring does not by itself
mean all of its stored records have been erased. Records can remain until they
are deleted through a request or service maintenance. Backups and technical logs
may remain until the hosting provider's configured retention periods expire;
information required for legal or security reasons may be kept longer.

We restrict server access using account authentication and room access rules.
The hosted service uses encrypted network connections. Local development builds
may use a developer's local network instead of the hosted service. No storage
or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

## Your choices and deletion requests

You can play solo without creating an account, choose whether to use Google
sign-in, leave multiplayer rooms and sign out on your device. Signing out does
not delete an account. Removing app data does not delete server records.

To request access, correction or deletion of your Match Rush account and its
associated personal data, email [bssam2012@gmail.com](mailto:bssam2012@gmail.com).
For deletion, use the subject **Match Rush account deletion** and include the
Google email address associated with your Match Rush account, or your guest
player identifier if available. Do not send your Google password, authentication
tokens or other credentials. We may ask for reasonable verification before
acting to prevent someone else from deleting your account.

Deletion requests are handled through this contact channel; the current app
does not have an automated account-deletion button. We will process verified
requests and explain any information that must be retained for a legal or
security reason. Deleting Match Rush data does not delete your Google account.
Depending on your location, you may also have rights to a copy of your data,
restriction or objection to processing, and to complain to your local privacy
regulator. Contact us to exercise applicable rights.

## Children and parents

The app does not ask players for their date of birth. If you believe a child
has provided personal information without the authorization required in your
location, contact us so we can investigate and handle a deletion request.
Parents or guardians can use the same privacy contact channel for relevant
questions or requests.
